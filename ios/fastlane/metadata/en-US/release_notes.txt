Ten Go has a fresh new look.

- A more polished design: buttons, cards and badges now have depth, and text is easier to read.
- Redesigned Progress screen: level, stats, daily missions and achievements in one place. Claim mission rewards right there.
- Clearer Duels: start a challenge or answer a code in one tap, and see your duels with their results.
- Puzzles: one button takes you straight to the next level.
- In a game, your score now sits at the top of the screen.
- Shop: prices appear in your currency, and every purchase clearly confirms its outcome.
