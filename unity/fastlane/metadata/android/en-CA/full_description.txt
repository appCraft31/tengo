Ten Go is the make 10 number puzzle: connect bubbles that add up to exactly 10 and watch the grid clear.

Easy to learn, deep to master. Swipe across neighboring bubbles from 1 to 9: 3+7, 2+5+3, 1+1+2+6… The longer the chain, the more it scores. Long chains make flowers bloom, and every flower is worth ×3.

HOW TO PLAY
• Draw a chain through adjacent bubbles
• The sum must be exactly 10
• Longer chains score far more points
• Go for ×3 flowers and clear the grid for a perfect bonus

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Free play: no timer, at your own pace
• Daily Challenge: the same grid for everyone
• Rush: 60 seconds of pure mental math
• Duel: challenge a friend on the exact same grid, each of you plays whenever you like
• Puzzles: 20 grids to solve, each with a guaranteed solution
