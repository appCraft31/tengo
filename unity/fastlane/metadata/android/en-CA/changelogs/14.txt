Ten Go has a fresh new look:
• a more polished design: buttons, cards and badges with depth, easier-to-read text;
• redesigned Progress screen, with daily missions you can claim right there;
• clearer duels, with their results;
• puzzles: one button takes you to the next level;
• in a game, the score moves to the top of the screen;
• the shop is now a single page, with prices in your currency.
