TEN·GO — The brain puzzle that sharpens your mind

TEN·GO is a minimalist puzzle built around strategy and calculation.
One grid. One rule: connect bubbles that add up to 10.
The longer your chain, the higher your score.

Plan your moves. Think ahead. Master the grid.

Sound and feel that reward your thinking

Every correct chain is answered by a note — your strategy becomes a melody:
• A soft electric piano synthesized in real time: each bubble is a note, each
  path a musical phrase
• 54 possible soundscapes across 6 musical scales — major and minor pentatonic,
  Hirajoshi, Kumoi, In Sen, hemitonic — never the same twice in a row
• Vibrations synchronized with the music: the phone responds to your notes,
  not just your finger
• A pastel palette that keeps the grid sharp and readable at a glance

Clear mechanics, deep strategy

• Slide your finger over neighboring bubbles (rows, columns, diagonals)
• The sum must be exactly 10 — no more, no less
• The longer the chain, the more points it's worth
• Clear the entire grid for a perfect bonus

Play at your own pace

No timer. No blocked levels. No energy to recharge.
Five minutes between meetings or an hour of deep focus — TEN·GO adapts.
Just the satisfaction of connecting, calculating, mastering.
