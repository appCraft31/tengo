TEN·GO — The zen puzzle to breathe

TEN·GO is a minimalist puzzle designed for calm. No timer. No stress.
Just you, a grid of soft-colored bubbles, and the simplest rule in the world:
find the sum of 10.

Connect neighboring bubbles whose numbers add up to exactly 10. They dissolve in a
harmonic wave, the bubbles above fall gently, and the grid breathes again.
Keep going until everything is free.

An experience designed for serenity

Every detail is calibrated to soothe you:
• A pastel palette inspired by watercolors
• Floating background bubbles, like a visual breath
• A soft electric piano synthesized in real time: each bubble is a note, each
  path a melody
• 54 possible soundscapes across 6 zen scales — major and minor pentatonic,
  Hirajoshi, Kumoi, In Sen, hemitonic — never the same twice in a row
• Even when playing fast, notes cascade gently without aggressive stacking
• Vibrations synchronized with the music: the phone pulses to the rhythm of your
  notes, not your finger
• No notifications, no urgent reminders

A game that adapts to your pace

Five minutes to unwind between appointments, or an hour to let yourself be absorbed
by the silence. No blocked levels, no energy to recharge, no life system. Just the
simple pleasure of connecting, adding, releasing.

Clear mechanics

• Slide your finger over neighboring bubbles (rows, columns, diagonals)
• The sum must be exactly 10 — no more, no less
• The longer the chain, the more points it's worth
• Clear the entire grid for a perfect bonus

Crafted with care

TEN·GO is a handcrafted project, designed with precision: minimalist interface,
fluid animations, portrait mode for a natural one-handed grip — on the subway, in
bed, by the fire. Sound, vibrations and tutorial can be adjusted in one tap from
the settings menu.
